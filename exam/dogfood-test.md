## Section 2: Edge Cases

10. [MC] What is $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$ called? [2pts]
a) Median
b) Mode
c) Sample mean [x]
d) Variance

11. [TF] The p-value is the probability that $H_0$ is true. [1pt]
a) True
b) False [x]

> A common misconception. The p-value is the probability of observing data as extreme as the sample, assuming $H_0$ is true.
